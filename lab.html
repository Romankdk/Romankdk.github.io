    How I work an alert

    My triage process

    The steps I follow on any alert, whether it comes from Splunk at work or from my honeypot at home.

    1. ValidateConfirm the alert is real and not a known false positive. Check the rule logic and the raw event.
    2. ScopePivot on user, host, IP, and hash in the SIEM to find every related event.
    3. EnrichCheck reputation (VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB) and map the behavior to MITRE ATT&CK.
    4. Contain or escalateFollow the playbook: block, isolate, or hand off to Tier 2 with clear notes.
    5. DocumentWrite the ticket so the next analyst understands what happened in two minutes.
